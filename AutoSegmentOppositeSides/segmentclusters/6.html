Triangulation matrix


This table represents a sorted triangulation matrix of the overlapping segments for the matches linked to this segment cluster. Using the triangulation data, a clustering is performed to identify clusters of triangulating segments. In some cases, all segments triangulate and the triangulation matrix will appear fully filled and colored green. In other cases, two groups of segments are found, one representing each parental side. Note that it is possible that a group consists of a single segment. The numbers in the matrix indicate a triangulation and show the overlap of two segments. In some cases, when there is more than one segment cluster, the segment clusters do not split into two groups. Instead, various segment clusters might appear. This is more often the case when examining smaller segments.
In some cases, the overlap reported in the matrix is larger as the overlap that is expected. In that case, the two DNA matches share a larger segment as compared to the segment that they share with you.



Admixture results


AutoSegment Split offers a detailed breakdown of admixture results for individual DNA segments, providing insights at a finer level than traditional admixture analyses. Typically, admixture results are presented per chromosome, but our tool goes deeper, analyzing specific segments of each chromosome. This allows users to understand the genetic contributions from different ancestral populations across distinct regions of their genome, revealing how diverse ancestries are distributed along their chromosomes. This detailed view can help uncover ancestral connections that might otherwise remain hidden in broader chromosome-wide analyses.
